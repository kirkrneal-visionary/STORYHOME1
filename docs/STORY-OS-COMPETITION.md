## 7. Strategic conclusion (truth)

1. **Do not out-Zillow Zillow** on national listings. Out-**trust** and out-**territory** them in East Texas.  
2. **Do not out-Instagram Instagram** on feed dopamine. Out-**continuity** them for market work.  
3. **Do not out-hype analytics startups** on predictions. Out-**evidence** them until scenarios are earned.  
4. **10 steps ahead** = Story OS (shell + temperatures + objects + honesty + RLS) so every new county, scenario, and module feels pre-installed — while CAD truth stays courtroom-safe.
