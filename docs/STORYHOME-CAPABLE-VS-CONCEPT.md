## The true concept (what we’re building)

**One continuous East Texas housing environment:**

1. **Consumers** browse homes and *belong* (My Home, saved, following) — not a throwaway listing dump.  
2. **Story Pro agents** work listings and CRM as their daily desk.  
3. **Archie’s Intelligence** is the pro-only study: county tax-roll truth, territories, traffic/growth evidence, private pipelines — **honest**, never fake “will sell” AI scores.  
4. Everything feels like **one product** (Story OS): you walk rooms of the same market, maps stay sacred, consumer never accidentally gets full Archie.

**Positioning in one line:**  
Story Home is an East Texas marketplace + agent OS; Archie is the county-records research cockpit that out-trusts national portals and out-evidences analytics startups — without out-Zillowing Zillow or out-hyping predictions.

## Part A — What the site is actually capable of today

### A1. Consumer / public (real, but shallower than the vision)

| Capability | Reality |
|---|---|
| Browse listings | Works — list/map, filters, drawn boundary, listing detail |
| Agent directory (Network) | Works — profiles, basic filters |
| Save / follow / My Home vault | Works for logged-in consumers |
| Share home docs with an agent | Partial — Client Homes path exists |
| Belonging Continuum (“walking the market”) | Designed in constitution; **not yet felt as a finished product experience** on every route |
| Consumer county intelligence | **Intentionally none** — Archie is Pro-only |

### A2. Story Pro desk (usable daily tools)

| Capability | Reality |
|---|---|
| Listings create/edit, statuses, seller codes | Works |
| MLS paste import + limited CAD pin for listing | Works (listing CAD ≠ full Archie) |
| Buyers / Sellers CRM pipelines | Works |
| Mortgage + cap-rate calculators | Works (local math) |
| Community (channels / knowledge / Q&A) | Present as Pro community surface |
| Messages | **Shell** — inbox theater, not a finished product |
| Referrals | **Shell** — columns/filters; pipeline not really live |
| Seller passcode portal | Exists; analytics/boost **claims outrun capture** |

### A3. Archie’s Intelligence (the strongest, most real surface)

**Launch footprint:** 7 East Texas counties — Polk, Angelina, Trinity, Tyler, San Jacinto, Liberty, Walker.

#### Research
- County-first search + MapLibre parcel map  
- Full property record from CAD (owner, situs, acres, values, school, MH when present)  
- Owner matches: EXACT vs POSSIBLE kept separate  
- Market Frames: draw → analyze parcels (counts, acres, median CAD)  
- Ownership Stability Index (300–850) — churn of CAD owner fields between Archie’s pulls (**not** credit / will-sell / deeds)  
- County observation feed — what changed between CAD loads  
- Evidence lane: strength chips, value trajectory, lookalike CAD band, illustrative payment under *your* assumptions  
- Discover: Find Similar + owner portfolio → map pins → bulk Prospects / Farm  

#### Prospects
- Private opportunity pipeline on public parcels  
- Status, tags, notes, activity  
- Convert to Seller Lead without inventing phone/email  

#### Farms
- Saved territories from analyzed frames  
- “Since your last review” vs live CAD  
- Mark reviewed; hand off into Research  

#### Study Vault
- Saved studies / frames; reopen into Research  

#### Corridors (finish set — live)
- **Traffic tool** — TxDOT AADT stations, ≥5-year history, corridor lines (planning counts, **not** live Google congestion)  
- **Growth watch** — evidence boxes with reasons (rising/high AADT + optional CAD pulse)  
- **Scenario board** — agent assumptions → labeled ranges + meeting pack print  
- **Land loop** — watch box → Research frame; TxDOT Project Tracker lines nearby  
- **Presentation mode** + **map pack** print for investor rooms  
- **Traffic memory** — this-browser “since we last looked” station snapshots  

### A4. What the system honestly stores / does *not* do

**Does store well:** CAD parcels + observation events; agent-private Prospects / Farms / Studies; CRM & listings business data.

**Does not have yet:** real product analytics (funnels, Archie usage, conversion); generative AI; seller-probability models; deed/MLS sale history as county truth; multi-device synced traffic memory; live congestion feeds.

**Forbidden by product law (and correctly absent):** fake AVM “true value,” will-sell scores, phone scraping from CAD, writing county CAD from agent workflows.

## Part B — Gap analysis: what must be added to *feel* the true concept

Corridors is **done as a module**. The remaining gap is not “one more Archie tool” — it’s making the **whole Story Home** feel like the concept above.

### Tier 1 — Feel like one product (highest leverage)

| Gap | Why it blocks the concept |
|---|---|
| **Continuum visibility** | Concept = walk the market. Today many transitions still feel like “reload a page,” not belonging. |
| **Finish or hide Messages & Referrals** | Nav promises social coordination; shells break trust (“is any of this real?”). |
| **Observation / empty-state honesty (OBS-OPS)** | Stability + change feed can look “broken” or “quiet market” when ops/migrations/refreshes aren’t ready — kills Archie’s credibility. |
| **CAD refresh as routine ops** | Archie’s memory *is* the moat; without regular county pulls, Farms/Stability/feed go stale. |

### Tier 2 — Make Pro + consumer sides feel intentional

| Gap | Why it blocks the concept |
|---|---|
| **Seller portal truth** | Either wire real view/click capture or tone down analytics — concept is honesty everywhere. |
| **Analytics foundation** | Can’t steer product or prove value without funnel + Archie usage (privacy-reviewed). |
| **Consumer depth** | Marketplace/My Home must feel like a home, not a thin listing wrapper next to a brilliant Pro study. |
| **Discover as first-class** | Buried inside Research; concept is a full intelligence room system, not a buried submenu. |

### Tier 3 — Scale the moat (after the product feels whole)

| Gap | Why it matters later |
|---|---|
| **More counties** | Concept is East Texas environment — 7 is a beachhead, not the map. |
| **Server-synced research memory** | Traffic/look memory across devices/agents (today browser-local for traffic). |
| **Assumption boards beyond Corridors** | Rate/price/rent scenarios on honesty ladder — still labeled, never oracles. |
| **Optional models** | Only *on top of* observation history + coverage confidence — never skip evidence. |
| **Live traffic (paid)** | Optional; concept does **not** require it. Planning AADT + projects is already the honest land-dev story. |

### Explicitly *not* required for the true concept

- National Zillow-scale listing coverage  
- Instagram-style feed addiction  
- Black-box seller AI  
- Pretending CAD = deeds or MLS comps

## Part D — Verdict for advisors

**What you have:** A real, differentiated Pro intelligence product (Research → Corridors → Prospects → Farms → Vault) on seven counties, plus a working marketplace and CRM desk — with an unusually clean honesty contract.

**What you don’t have yet:** The *feeling* that the whole site is one continuous Story Home environment. The weak links are shell theater (Messages/Referrals), Continuum belonging, observation empty-state honesty, seller analytics truth, and zero product telemetry.

**True-concept finish order (product sense, not a build ticket):**  
1) Continuum visibility + kill shell theater  
2) OBS-OPS + CAD refresh reliability  
3) Analytics + seller truth  
4) Consumer belonging depth  
5) County expansion / optional deeper scenarios  

**Corridors itself:** that chapter is complete for the intended land-developer / traffic thesis. Next energy belongs to **Story OS as a whole**, not another Corridors wave.
